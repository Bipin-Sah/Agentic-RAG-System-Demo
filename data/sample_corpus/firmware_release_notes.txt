Zephyr Fleet Firmware - Release Notes

3.3.0 (October 2024)
- Adds obstacle avoidance for low-light warehouses.
- Battery health estimation now reports remaining charge cycles.

3.2.2 (14 July 2024)
- Hotfix: corrects leap-second handling in the GNSS time-synchronisation module (see INC-2291).

3.2.1 (June 2024)
- Improves landing precision on charging pads to +/- 2 cm.
- Known issue: navigation fixes may be rejected after a leap-second correction.

3.2.0 (April 2024)
- First firmware release supporting the Zephyr X2.
- New fleet dashboard integration.

3.1.0 (January 2024)
- Final feature release for the Zephyr X1. Later X1 releases contain security fixes only.
