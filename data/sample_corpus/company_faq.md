# Zephyr Robotics – Company FAQ

## Who are we?
Zephyr Robotics builds autonomous inspection and delivery drones for warehouses and industrial
sites. The company was founded in 2017 by Mara Lindqvist and Tomas Okafor. Mara Lindqvist is the
current CEO and Tomas Okafor serves as Chief Technology Officer.

## Where are we located?
Our headquarters are in Tallinn, Estonia. We also operate a warehouse and test facility in
Lisbon, Portugal, and a sales office in Austin, Texas.

## How big is the company?
As of January 2025 Zephyr Robotics employs 340 people across all locations.
