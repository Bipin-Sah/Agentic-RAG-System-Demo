Zephyr Robotics Customer Support and Warranty Policy

Warranty
The Zephyr X2 is covered by a 24-month limited hardware warranty from the date of delivery.
The older Zephyr X1 is covered by a 12-month limited warranty. Batteries are covered for 300 charge
cycles or 12 months, whichever comes first. Damage caused by crashes, water immersion or
unauthorised repairs is not covered.

Support tiers
Standard support is included with every purchase. Standard tickets receive a first response
within 2 business days.
Priority support costs $899 per drone per year. Priority tickets receive a first response within
4 hours, 24/7, and include one free replacement battery per year.

Returns
Unused drones may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
