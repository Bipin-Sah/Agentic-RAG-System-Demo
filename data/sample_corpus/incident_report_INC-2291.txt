INCIDENT REPORT INC-2291
Status: Closed
Severity: SEV-2

Summary
On 14 July 2024 the entire drone fleet at the Lisbon warehouse was grounded after drones began
reporting position errors of up to 800 metres. 42 drones were affected and warehouse inspection
operations were halted for 6 hours.

Timeline
06:10 - First GPS drift alerts raised by the fleet monitoring system.
06:25 - Operations lead grounds all drones at the Lisbon site.
09:40 - Root cause identified by the firmware team.
12:10 - Firmware hotfix deployed and fleet returned to service.

Root cause
Firmware version 3.2.1 contained a bug in leap-second handling in the GNSS time-synchronisation
module. When the receiver applied a pending leap-second correction, the navigation filter
rejected valid satellite fixes.

Resolution
The bug was fixed in firmware version 3.2.2, which was rolled out to the Lisbon fleet the same day
and to all other customer fleets within one week.

Investigation lead: Priya Raman (Senior Firmware Engineer)

Follow-up actions
1. Add leap-second scenarios to the hardware-in-the-loop test suite.
2. Require staged rollouts (5% of fleet first) for all navigation firmware.
